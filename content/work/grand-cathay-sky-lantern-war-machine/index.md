<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-2">🔥 Use Case: Flying Centrepiece</h3>
<p class="text-gray-300 leading-relaxed">
The Sky Lantern drifts over the battlefield on an ivory paper hull caged in red silk rope, vermilion dragons stencilled across every panel. Slung beneath is a red-lacquer war-junk gondola bristling with rockets and cannon — and a full crew of firebomb bombardiers and an Astromancer Lord, each model dismounted here so the detail packed into this war machine reads from every angle.
</p>

Tactical Protocol
</h4>
<span class="text-[10px] uppercase text-gray-500">Methodology</span>
</div>
<p class="text-sm text-gray-400 mb-2">Key techniques deployed:</p>
<ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300 list-disc list-inside marker:text-cyan-500">
<li><strong>Freehand Stencils:</strong> Vermilion dragons painted directly onto the bone paper panels, each one mirrored across the hull — no transfers.</li>
<li><strong>Paper Translucency:</strong> Bone glazed thin over the ribbed panels to read as taut lantern paper rather than solid plastic.</li>
<li><strong>Crew Sub-Assembly:</strong> Every bombardier and the Astromancer Lord painted off the model, then seated into the gondola for a clean, layered finish.</li>
</ul>
