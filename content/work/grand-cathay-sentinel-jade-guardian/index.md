<p class="text-gray-300 leading-relaxed">
A Cathayan Sentinel rendered as living jade — cold green-teal stone that reads as carved relic rather than painted plastic. Dense cloud-relief covers armour and twin blades, sapphire gems burn in chest and helm, and the weathered stone dais roots it in the ruins of an old Cathayan gate. A deliberate break from the standard red-gold Cathay palette.

<span class="text-[10px] uppercase text-gray-500">Methodology</span>
</div>
<p class="text-sm text-gray-400 mb-2">Key techniques deployed:</p>
<ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300 list-disc list-inside marker:text-cyan-500">
<li><strong>Jade Layering:</strong> Sotek Green built up to Temple Guard Blue with cold grey edges so every panel reads as polished stone, not armour.</li>
<li><strong>Relief Carving:</strong> Cloud motifs pushed with thin highlight lines to simulate bas-relief cut into the jade surface.</li>
<li><strong>Oxide Washes:</strong> Nihilakh Oxide run into recesses to age the joints like a statue left standing for centuries.</li>
<li><strong>Gem Facets:</strong> Caledor Sky gems capped with a single bright point to catch light like inset sapphires.</li>
</ul>
