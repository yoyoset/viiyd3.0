share_caption: "Eight Chaos Terminators, sixty-four photos, and a lot of gold trim.

Black Legion is one of those schemes that looks simple until you actually sit down to edge every Chaos trim plate on eight chunky Terminator suits. For this commission we kept the armour black with crisp grey edge highlights, warm burnished gold across the trims, bone-toned tusks and skulls, and a mix of Ultramarines blue, Dark Angels green, and Blood Angels red on the helmets spiked to their trophy racks.

One squad champion goes bare-headed with an accursed sword, alongside a Reaper autocannon gunner and a pair of lightning claws, all finished on textured wasteland bases with grass tufts and small blooms. Black-on-black always eats light on the camera, so getting the metallic contrast to pop without blowing out the ivory took a bit of care.

If you have a Chaos squad sitting on the sprue and want to see how far we can take it, send over a few photos and we can figure out the scheme together."
