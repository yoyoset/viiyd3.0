share_caption: "Not every army needs to be a showpiece. Sometimes you have a box of Ultramarines, a tournament date and a limited budget, and the honest answer is block colour.

The squads and the hero units in this batch got exactly that: a clean, even coat of Ultramarine blue over the armour, white on the legs and helmets where a unit carries it, black on the weapons and bases, red and gold only where a model has them, and nothing else. No wash in the recesses, no edge highlights. It is the cheapest thing I do, cheaper even than a basic flat paint job with a wash, and the point is that it is still a proper army: one blue, applied the same way on every model, so the squads read as one force from across the table.

What I like is how uniform it comes out. When the blue is smooth and the coverage is complete, even a plain scheme looks deliberate rather than unfinished.

The trade-off is depth. Without shading the sculpted detail on the armour sits flatter than it would on a fuller job, and up close you notice it. If you want that depth later, these are exactly the models that can take it on top. Send me photos of yours and we can talk about how far to take it."
