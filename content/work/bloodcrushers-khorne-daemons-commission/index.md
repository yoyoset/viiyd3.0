<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-2">Use Case: Daemon Cavalry Unit</h3>
<p class="text-gray-300 leading-relaxed">
This commission called for six Bloodcrushers painted as a single charging unit, so the palette had to hold up at both single-model and full-formation range. The bloodletter riders keep a deep, saturated red skin, while the juggernaut mounts carry brass and steel plating that reads clearly against the red without competing with it.
</p>

Tactical Protocol
</h4>
<span class="text-[10px] uppercase text-gray-500">Methodology</span>
</div>
<p class="text-sm text-gray-400 mb-2">Key techniques deployed:</p>
<ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300 list-disc list-inside marker:text-cyan-500">
<li><strong>Unit-Wide Consistency:</strong> Six models were batch-shaded and highlighted together so the red skin tone stays identical across the whole formation.</li>
<li><strong>Brass vs. Steel Split:</strong> Juggernaut plating was separated into warm brass panels and cool steel edges to keep the armor readable at a glance.</li>
<li><strong>Weathered Leather:</strong> Harness straps were given worn edge highlights to break up the large red and brass surfaces with texture.</li>
</ul>
