<p class="text-gray-300 leading-relaxed">
Twenty-four levied peasants called from the fields — red tunics, ivory cloth and blue belts reading instantly as a Cathayan battleline, while straw hats, wooden shafts and iron farm tools tell the conscript story. The colour lock keeps the mass coherent; the faces, poses and simple weapons keep each model alive.

Tactical Protocol
</h4>
<span class="text-[10px] uppercase text-gray-500">Methodology</span>
</div>
<p class="text-sm text-gray-400 mb-2">Key techniques deployed:</p>
<ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300 list-disc list-inside marker:text-cyan-500">
<li><strong>Regimental Colour Lock:</strong> All twenty-four held to one red-ivory-blue recipe so the mass reads as a single levied host.</li>
<li><strong>Individual Variation:</strong> Different skin tones, hair and weapon grips keep the repeated bodies from reading as clones.</li>
<li><strong>Tool-to-Weapon Weathering:</strong> Iron toolheads given medium wear — old steel, not parade-polished armour.</li>
<li><strong>Metal Zone Washes:</strong> Nuln on iron, Agrax on leather and wood, keeping cheap materials earthy and separate.</li>
</ul>
