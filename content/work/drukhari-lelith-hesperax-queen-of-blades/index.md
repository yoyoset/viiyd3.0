share_caption: "Lelith Hesperax, one model, and honestly the hardest part was the hair.

A topknot this size is basically a second sculpture. From most angles it is one flat red, so the shape has to carry it, which means shading the folds of the ponytail instead of trusting the colour to do something clever. The pale grey skin sits right against the black bodysuit and that is a thin line to walk: too much contrast and the face disappears, too little and the whole model goes flat.

The base gave me the story. She is landing on a fallen gilded statue, and keeping that gold dulled and dirty is what makes the leap read as something happening in an arena rather than on a plinth.

If you want a character who looks like she is mid-move rather than posed, that is my favourite thing to paint."

Tactical Protocol
</h4>
<span class="text-[10px] uppercase text-gray-500">Methodology</span>
</div>
<p class="text-sm text-gray-400 mb-2">Key techniques deployed:</p>
<ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300 list-disc list-inside marker:text-cyan-500">
<li><strong>Sculpted Hair:</strong> The topknot is shaded as a solid form, with the folds of each strand carved by shadow rather than a change of colour.</li>
<li><strong>Pale Skin Against Black:</strong> Administratum Grey built up to Pallid Wych Flesh, with the bodysuit kept matte so the skin stays the brightest thing on the model.</li>
<li><strong>Blade Edges:</strong> Leadbelcher darkened in the flats and brought up to Stormhost Silver only on the cutting edges.</li>
<li><strong>Aged Gold:</strong> The fallen statue is painted with Averland Sunset and then knocked back with shading and grime so it reads as forgotten, not polished.</li>
</ul>
