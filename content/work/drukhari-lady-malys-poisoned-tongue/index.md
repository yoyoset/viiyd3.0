share_caption: "Lady Malys, one model, built around a fan of blades that had to read as elegant before it read as dangerous.

Drukhari armour is a trap. Go too dark and the whole model turns into one black shape, so the teal is kept cold and clean and the gold trim does the work of separating every plate. The blade fan is the part I kept coming back to: bone blades, gold barbs, and a pose that only lands if the spread looks effortless.

Painting an Archon is mostly about resisting the urge to add more. She already has the fan, the cloak and the braid, and the real job was keeping all three readable at the same time.

If you have a character who is meant to look in control rather than furious, that is the kind of brief I like getting."

<span class="text-[10px] uppercase text-gray-500">Methodology</span>
</div>
<p class="text-sm text-gray-400 mb-2">Key techniques deployed:</p>
<ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300 list-disc list-inside marker:text-cyan-500">
<li><strong>Cold Teal Layering:</strong> Stegadon Scale Green built up through Sotek Green so the armour stays blue-cold instead of tipping green.</li>
<li><strong>Gold Separation:</strong> Every plate is edged in gold so the silhouette reads at a distance without relying on a wash.</li>
<li><strong>Cloak Depth:</strong> Black cloak glazed toward Mechanicus Standard Grey on the folds, with the olive lining kept flat so it reads as a second surface.</li>
<li><strong>Bone Blades:</strong> The fan is kept off-white with warm shading so it stands apart from the teal and gold around it.</li>
</ul>
