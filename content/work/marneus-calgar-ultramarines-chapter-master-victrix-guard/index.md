Marneus Calgar and a Victrix Guard are both mostly one big blue field, so the work is in keeping it from going flat: highlights on the edges, a darker value in the recesses, and the gold and the red cape doing the talking everywhere else. The cape is the part I would point at first. It is the largest bit of red on either model, and it has to read as cloth that moves, not as a red slab.

Calgar stands on a cracked slab with chains and trophies around his feet, so the base has its own small story. The Guard is cleaner on purpose: plume, shield, sword, nothing extra.
