Tactical Protocol

<span class="text-[10px] uppercase text-gray-500">Methodology</span>
</div>
<p class="text-sm text-gray-400 mb-2">Key techniques deployed:</p>
<ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300 list-disc list-inside marker:text-cyan-500">
<li><strong>Cross-Unit Colour Lock:</strong> Gunners and crane crews share one recipe so both units deploy as a single fire-support wing.</li>
<li><strong>Barrel Depth:</strong> Dark Eshin Grey underpaint under steel so gun barrels read long and heavy, not flat silver.</li>
<li><strong>Metal Zone Washes:</strong> Nuln on steel, Agrax on gold — separate recessing keeps mechanical contrast high.</li>
<li><strong>Action Variety:</strong> Different loading, aiming and bracing poses across the twelve give the battery a working-crew feel.</li>
</ul>
