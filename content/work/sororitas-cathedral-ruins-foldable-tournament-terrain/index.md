share_caption: "This time it was not troops on the desk but a whole table: two-storey ruins, low line walls, containers, and a handful of scatter pieces.

Terrain can go wrong in two ways: it upstages the armies, or it blurs into one mass. So the stone is all one cool grey, and every bit of trim and every door is copper, which means you can tell wall from door and window at a glance from across the table. The statues and plaques are left white, and the white fleur-de-lis on red only appears on the banners and the containers. There is not much red, but it is what makes the whole table read as Sororitas.

The set is foldable 3D-printed terrain, and up close the layer lines are still visible. Paint cannot hide that, and I would rather tell you so plainly.

If you have a box of printed terrain at home that has stayed bare all this time, you are welcome to send photos and talk it over. Painting the whole table together comes out far more consistent than touching up pieces one at a time."
