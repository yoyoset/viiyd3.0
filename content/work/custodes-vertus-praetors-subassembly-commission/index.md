<h3 class="text-xl font-bold text-white mb-2">Use Case: Sub-Assembly Showcase</h3>
<p class="text-gray-300 leading-relaxed">
This commission was built around controlled access: wings, riders, lances, and jetbike sections were painted before final assembly so the gold armor and silver mechanical surfaces stayed crisp. The result keeps the classic Custodes palette, but gives each component enough contrast to read clearly before the models are fully mounted.

<span class="text-[10px] uppercase text-gray-500">Methodology</span>
</div>
<p class="text-sm text-gray-400 mb-2">Key techniques deployed:</p>
<ul class="space-y-2 text-sm text-gray-300 list-disc list-inside marker:text-cyan-500">
<li><strong>Sub-Assembly Painting:</strong> Kept wing feathers, shoulder scrollwork, and underside details reachable before final mounting.</li>
<li><strong>Controlled Metallic Contrast:</strong> Warm gold armor was separated from cooler silver wings and weapon parts to avoid a flat all-metal read.</li>
<li><strong>Spot Color Discipline:</strong> Red cloth and blue gems were used as small anchors so the eye has a path through the gold surfaces.</li>
</ul>
